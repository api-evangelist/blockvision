Many of you have reported issues with the rate limit for our public endpoint. Please be aware that the public endpoint is accessible to the general public, which means that many others can use it simultaneously, potentially leading to reaching the rate limit. If you require a private endpoint, you can proceed to the BlockVision dashboard to select your preferred plan (ranging from free to Pro, based on your usage) and configure it in your wallet.
